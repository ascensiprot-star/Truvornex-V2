# Privacy and GDPR Engineering Requirements

Engineering checklist only; not legal advice or substitute for DPO/qualified counsel.

Before processing identify controller/processor roles, purpose, legal basis, data categories, subjects, recipients, retention, international transfers, and special-category/criminal-offence data. Assess DPIA necessity for systematic monitoring, large-scale sensitive data, or consequential profiling.

Implement purpose limitation, minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, transparency, integrity/confidentiality, accountability, privacy by default, and data-subject rights. Consent is not a universal legal basis and must not be used as a shortcut where inappropriate.

Support access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, objection, and consent withdrawal when relevant. Verify, scope, track, and document requests and lawful exceptions. Maintain processor/subprocessor register, contracts, security reviews, data locations, transfer mechanisms, deletion/return terms, and incident obligations.

Engineering controls: field minimization; encryption; access segregation; audit; retention automation; export; deletion/anonymization; privacy-safe analytics; no sensitive data in logs; synthetic test fixtures.
