# Data Classification, Access, Retention, and Deletion

Classifications: **Public** (intentionally public listings/policies); **Internal** (operational metadata); **Confidential** (business, transaction, organization data); **Sensitive personal** (identity, location, private messages, credentials); **Restricted financial/security** (payment references, fraud/security data, auth metadata, secrets).

For each data item define purpose, owner, subject, source, collection time, legal-basis assessment, recipients, sharing scope, retention class, deletion/anonymization rule, export behavior, encryption/access policy, cross-border transfer assessment, and audit requirement.

Do not invent one retention period for everything. Define jurisdiction/document-specific schedules for tax/accounting, disputes/legal holds, fraud/security, and contract. Counsel validates. At expiry delete, anonymize, or restrict as specified.

Support access, correction, portability where applicable, objection/restriction, erasure, and consent withdrawal where relevant. Verify requests, track them, and explain lawful retention exceptions. Do not collect exact location, contacts, device IDs, identity scans, or message bodies by default. Device permission is not blanket permission for unrelated processing.
