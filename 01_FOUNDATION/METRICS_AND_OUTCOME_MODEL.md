# Metrics and Outcome Model

## North-star outcome

**Verified successful economic workflows completed by active participants**, segmented by country, workflow type, participant size, and cohort. Count only when fulfillment criteria are met and the outcome is not later invalidated by fraud, cancellation, or unresolved dispute.

## Network health

Activated eligible supply; genuine demand; share of requests receiving relevant available offers; time to qualified response; time to agreement; fulfillment completion/cancellation rates with reason codes; repeat transactions; buyer/provider retention; category and geographic liquidity.

## Trust and quality

Verification coverage by evidence type; stale credential rate; substantiated fraud; disputes per completed order; resolution time; appeal reversal rate; review manipulation; false positives/negatives for risk controls.

## Economic utility

Participant savings/revenue enabled; provider utilization where measurable; coordination time per transaction; payment reconciliation success; invoice exception rate; platform fee relative to delivered value; SME onboarding burden.

## Reliability

Critical request success; API latency/error rates; mobile crash-free sessions; offline conflict rate; webhook lag; event-processing lag; restore-test success; accessibility/localization defects.

Do not optimize transaction count at the expense of safety/fairness. Do not use daily active users, messages, or screen time as proxies for economic value. Each metric needs numerator, denominator, owner, cohort, source, freshness, exclusions, and known bias.
