# Platform Governance and Fair Participation

Publish clear rules for onboarding, verification, listing quality, ranking, fees, moderation, disputes, suspension, reinstatement, data access, and policy changes. Give meaningful reasons and effective dates for material policy changes.

Separate platform decisions from AI recommendations. Provide human review/appeal for high-impact restrictions. Prevent conflicts of interest in dispute/trust review. Record operator access and decision rationale.

Assess whether qualification, ranking, price, language coverage, device requirements, and fraud controls unintentionally exclude smaller providers or participant cohorts. Do not weaken legitimate safety/legal standards; find less discriminatory ways to meet them. Establish user advisory and structured feedback process; maintain accountable owner/version history.
