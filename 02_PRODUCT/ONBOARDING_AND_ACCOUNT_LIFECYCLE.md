# Onboarding and Account Lifecycle

Onboarding should collect only what is needed for the next valuable task. A compulsory long wizard collecting region, interests, appearance, and preferences before explaining product value may create unnecessary abandonment; test progressive disclosure.

Lifecycle: visitor → account created → contact verified → profile initialized → optional identity evidence linked → optional organization membership → eligible for specific actions → active → restricted/suspended/closed.

Account creation is not legal identity verification. Email/phone confirmation is not government identity. A profile is not proof of business registration or professional licensing.

Collect country, residence, business establishment, service location, tax jurisdiction, and UI language as distinct fields where relevant. Ask for location/camera/contacts only at feature use with explanation and alternatives. Interests must be editable and collected only when useful.

Recovery must resist SIM swap, email compromise, social engineering, and device loss; it must not silently reduce identity assurance. High-risk changes require step-up verification and notifications. Account closure explains outstanding orders, disputes, invoices, and statutory retention; support lawful export and deletion/anonymization.
