# Simon — Intelligence and Assistance System

Simon helps users understand options, discover patterns, forecast permitted operations, and complete approved low-risk workflows. It is not the system of record or autonomous economic actor.

Candidate use cases: natural-language request structuring; query/search assistance; provider operational summaries; capacity/demand forecasts; anomaly triage; document extraction with human verification; multilingual assistance; workflow explanation; evidence-grounded recommendations.

Disallowed by default: issuing legal identity, approving qualifications, determining legal eligibility, final credit/employment/housing/insurance decisions, changing accepted terms, executing payments/refunds, altering ledger, suspending accounts without review, or exposing data beyond authorization.

Separate orchestration, retrieval, typed allowlisted tools, model adapters, policy evaluation, telemetry, and evaluation. Enforce tenant/organization permissions on retrieval and tools. Treat user text, documents, and provider content as untrusted against prompt injection.

Consequential outputs state evidence, freshness, uncertainty, limitations, and next verification. Distinguish fact from inference. Require human confirmation before external actions. Simon outage must not block discovery, agreements, orders, payments status, or disputes. Do not train on private messages, identity evidence, or sensitive docs by default. Define purpose, legal basis, retention, subprocessors, transfers, deletion, and controls.
