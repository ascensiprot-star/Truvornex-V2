# AI Governance and Risk Controls

Maintain an AI inventory with use case, intended purpose, model/provider version, affected actors, data classes, risk assessment, human oversight, transparency, evaluations, incidents, and jurisdiction/sector restrictions.

Classify each use case under applicable law before release. Classification depends on intended purpose/context, not the team's label. Obtain legal review when it may be high-risk or regulated.

Controls: minimization, authorization, retention, prompt-injection defenses, output validation, abuse tests, model change control, fallback, rollback. Evaluate hallucination, bias, privacy leakage, unsafe tool use, prompt injection, robustness, language quality, and cohort performance. Human review must be meaningful; preserve what Simon suggested versus what user/operator approved.
