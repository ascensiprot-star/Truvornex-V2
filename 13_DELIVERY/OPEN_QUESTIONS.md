# Open Questions Requiring Evidence or Decisions

## Market/product
- Which initial country/territory and sector have strongest verified pain and density?
- Which workflow has buyer, provider, repeat frequency, measurable value?
- Who pays: individual, provider, SME, institution, or combination?
- What is the existing manual workaround, and why is it insufficient?
- Which regulated service categories must be excluded initially?

## Business/operations
- Which legal entity operates in each market?
- Who handles support, disputes, fraud escalation, and legal requests?
- What are provider economics and concentration/exit risks?
- What are minimum category/region liquidity thresholds?

## Architecture
- Verify current repository and reusable components.
- Can existing SQLite backend be retired without data loss? Is there a canonical PostgreSQL backend?
- What measured concurrency, storage, latency, and availability requirements exist?
- What evidence would justify independent deployment of a module?

## Integrations/regulation
- Which EUDI profiles/wallets are production-ready in selected markets?
- Which PSP supports intended marketplace model and target countries?
- Which e-invoicing standards/access points/local mandates apply?
- Which DSA, NIS2, AI Act, platform-work, consumer, or other scopes apply to actual role and size?

## Simon
- Which one low-risk, measurable, high-value use case has lawful data?
- Does it need a model, deterministic rules, retrieval, or combination?
- What are human confirmation and evaluation thresholds?

Each question has owner, evidence required, status, and decision rationale. Do not invent answers.
