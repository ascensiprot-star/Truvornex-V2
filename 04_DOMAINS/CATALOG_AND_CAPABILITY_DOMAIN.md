# Catalog and Capability Domain

A listing describes what is offered, responsible actor, service area, constraints, price model/currency, availability, qualification requirements, cancellation terms, and evidence for relevant claims.

Use versioned taxonomy with localization separate from canonical IDs. Distinguish service category from legal classification; category does not prove permission to perform regulated work.

Lifecycle: draft → submitted → review where necessary → published → paused/expired/withdrawn. Material edits create new versions and may trigger re-verification or re-acceptance. A published listing does not imply current availability. Availability must come from explicit schedule/capacity state with freshness and conflict controls.
