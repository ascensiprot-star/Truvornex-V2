# Fulfillment Domain

Track performance against an accepted agreement: milestones, schedules, responsible parties, deliverables, evidence, completion claims, acceptance, rejection, remediation, and change requests.

Evidence may include participant confirmation, checklists, document references, timestamps, or partner statuses. Media evidence is purpose-limited, consent-aware, access-controlled, and retained only as required. Location evidence is not collected by default.

The receiving party can accept, reject with reason, or request correction under agreed rules. Define timeout/escalation explicitly. Never auto-accept because a notification was opened. Cancellation and failure are first-class flows with initiator, policy, consequences, and appeal path.
