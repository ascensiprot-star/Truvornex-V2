# Reputation and Reviews

Reputation is an evidence-presentation system, not hidden authority. Separate verified transaction history, fulfillment outcomes, participant feedback, qualifications, response behavior, and unresolved complaints.

Reviews must be tied to eligible interactions and protected from manipulation/retaliation. Provide moderation reasons and challenge paths. No purchased reviews or undisclosed paid ranking. Exclude sensitive personal data, protected characteristics, private messages, and unsupported allegations. Differentiate opinion from platform-verified fact. Show sample size/recency and avoid misleading statistics.

Do not present a single opaque universal score. Prefer transparent component indicators with definitions and limitations.
