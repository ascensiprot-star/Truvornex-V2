# Request, Offer, Agreement, and Order Domain

A **request** expresses a need. An **offer** proposes scope, price, timing, and conditions. An **agreement** records accepted terms and parties' authority. An **order** operationalizes an agreement for fulfillment. Do not collapse these into a generic booking.

Every issued offer version is immutable. Changes create a new version. Acceptance references the exact version and actor authority. Post-acceptance changes require an amendment/change order accepted by authorized parties.

Minimum agreement terms: parties; scope/deliverables; price/currency; taxes/fees where known; timing; location/jurisdiction; cancellation/refund rules; acceptance criteria; evidence; documents; data handling; dispute process; version and acceptance evidence.

Lifecycle transitions are guarded: draft → issued → viewed → accepted/rejected/expired/withdrawn; agreement activation only after valid acceptance/preconditions. Orders progress through scheduled, in progress, submitted, accepted, rejected, cancelled, disputed, or closed as appropriate.

Electronic acceptance, consumer/distance-contract rights, withdrawal, mandatory disclosures, and country-specific contract formation require legal review. A checkbox alone is not universal legal consent.
