# Verification and Trust Evidence Domain

Do not build one opaque universal trust score. Represent distinct dimensions: verified identity, business registration, issuer-checked qualification, completed transaction history, disputes, and stale/missing evidence. These claims are not interchangeable.

An evidence record includes subject, claimant, type, issuer/source, method, scope, jurisdiction, issue/expiry/last-check times, status, confidence/limitations, legal basis/consent reference, and access policy.

Statuses: `unsubmitted`, `pending`, `verified`, `rejected`, `expired`, `revoked`, `unable_to_verify`, `under_review`. Verified applies only to one claim/scope, not blanket endorsement.

Explain evidence behind consequential decisions, freshness, and challenge/appeal path. Automated risk signals are advisory unless a legally reviewed policy permits a narrow action. High-impact adverse decisions require meaningful human review. Avoid sensitive-attribute profiling and discriminatory proxies.
