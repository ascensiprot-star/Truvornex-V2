# Organization and Delegation Domain

Organizations are collaboration/authority contexts; legal entities are external legal constructs linked with provenance. A Truvornex organization is not automatically a legal entity.

Support creation, legal-entity linking, verification evidence, member invitation, role/capability assignment, delegated approvals, membership expiry/revocation, ownership changes, and audit history. Require additional review for ownership and payout-destination changes.

A grant records organization, grantee, permission, scope, grantor, expiry, and revocation. High-impact permissions require explicit confirmation by an authorized officer or provider-backed check. Account creator does not automatically retain permanent authority to bind the entity.

Offboarding revokes access promptly while preserving lawful records and historical attribution. Provide a controlled path for ownership/authority disputes.
