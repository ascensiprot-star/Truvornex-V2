# Disputes, Complaints, and Appeals

Support transaction disputes, service complaints, fraud reports, identity/credential challenges, content reports, account restrictions, and appeals as distinct case types.

Case record: ID/type, reporter/respondent, affected transaction/resource, allegation, evidence references, timestamps, jurisdiction, status, reviewer, conflict checks, actions, outcome rationale, appeal state, retention class.

Preserve evidence with integrity/access controls. Explain process and next step. Separate automated flags from findings. Give affected parties a chance to respond when safe and lawful. Protect reporter identity where justified. Require human review for consequential restrictions and complex cases. Record rationale and quality metrics.

Truvornex is not a court/regulator. Do not claim to determine legal rights beyond platform process. Preserve access to external remedies.
