# Communication and Notification Domain

Messaging supports transaction communication, references/attachments, delivery status, reporting, blocking, retention, and role access. It is not a general surveillance or engagement feed.

Messages are not contracts. A message does not amend an agreement unless an explicit amendment workflow captures acceptance. Notifications are hints, not authority: push may be delayed, dropped, or duplicated; fetch canonical server state before consequential actions.

Protect against spam, scams, harassment, and mass unsolicited contact through rate limits, reporting, blocking, and controlled evidence preservation. Define retention/moderation transparently. Do not expose private messages to Simon by default; AI message analysis requires purpose, user control, and privacy review.
