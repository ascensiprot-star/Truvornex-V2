# Discovery and Matching Domain

Search eligible services, providers, organizations, and resources. Filters may include capability, service area, availability, price model, evidence type, language, accessibility, and preferences. Search must not imply legal eligibility unless checked for that service and jurisdiction.

Separate hard constraints (legal eligibility, active status, availability, required qualification) from soft ranking (distance, response rate, preferences, price, quality evidence). Explain meaningful ranking factors and allow alternate sorting.

Monitor exposure/conversion across provider cohorts. Do not rank solely by pay-to-play or opaque historical engagement. Sponsored placements, if introduced, must be labeled and cannot override mandatory qualifications or safety filters.

When supply is insufficient, say so. Never fabricate providers, availability, ratings, or matches. Waitlist/request broadcast needs consent and rate limits. Feedback is evaluation data, not an automatic reputation penalty.
