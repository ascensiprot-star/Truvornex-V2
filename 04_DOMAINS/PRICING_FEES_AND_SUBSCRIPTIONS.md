# Pricing, Fees, and Subscription Domain

Pricing is explicit, versioned, jurisdiction-aware, and shown before acceptance. Distinguish provider price, platform fee, PSP fee, tax, discount, refund, and FX.

Subscriptions represent plan, entitlements, billing period, renewal, cancellation, proration, failure, grace period, and invoice. Do not condition fundamental privacy rights, appeals, data export, or essential safety access on paid plans unless a lawful justified model permits it.

Promotions/sponsored placement must be disclosed. Fee changes cannot retroactively alter accepted agreements. Revenue models are hypotheses; validate willingness to pay, fairness, unit economics, and regulation before shipping monetization.
