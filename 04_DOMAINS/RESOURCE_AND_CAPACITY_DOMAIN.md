# Resource and Capacity Domain

Represent people/time slots, equipment, facilities, vehicles where permitted, inventory, and operational capacity—not only listings. A resource has owner/controller, capability, location/service area, availability, constraints, relevant safety/eligibility checks, maintenance state, and allocation policy.

Capacity is finite and concurrent. Prevent double booking through transactional locking/version checks or another proven approach. Holds expire and release capacity safely. Model time zones and daylight-saving changes explicitly.

Human-labor workflows may create employment, worker-classification, agency, or platform-work obligations. Obtain legal review before designing features that direct, supervise, rank, or discipline workers.

Do not expose precise locations/schedules publicly where that creates privacy or safety risk. Use coarse location and controlled disclosure when appropriate.
