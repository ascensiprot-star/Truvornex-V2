# Country Launch Checklist

A country must be assessed independently. Geographic availability is not permission to transact.

- Determine territory: EU, EEA/EFTA, UK, Switzerland, or other.
- Check company establishment and participant legal requirements.
- Review consumer, marketplace, platform-to-business, e-commerce, and advertising law.
- Determine service/profession restrictions and qualification recognition.
- Review worker classification/platform-work implications.
- Confirm identity/eID, business-registry interfaces and legal basis.
- Confirm PSP coverage, currency, payment methods, payout, refunds, chargebacks.
- Review VAT, structured e-invoice, tax records/retention/reporting.
- Review privacy, transfers, localization, and regulator guidance.
- Review accessibility/national implementation.
- Validate languages, addresses, phone formats, time zones, and support.
- Test country-specific failure and dispute paths.
- Obtain qualified local legal/tax review and written launch approval.

Unsupported functions must be disabled for the affected territory and user context.
